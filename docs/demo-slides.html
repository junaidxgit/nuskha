Who it's for

Anyone holding an Indian prescription.

A parent, a patient, a caregiver. Two things are true about that piece of paper.

  • The brand prescribed is often several times the price of an identical composition — hospitals and pharmacies have commercial arrangements.
  • The batches that failed testing are already on a public government list. A list no patient has ever seen.

Why you can trust it

Every claim carries its source.

  • The authority levels never blend — a regulator's record is labelled a regulator's record.
  • An NSQ finding is batch-specific: one batch, one date, one stated reason. Never a statement about the company.
  • Absence of a record is not evidence of quality — only sampled batches are tested.
  • It never tells you to switch medication. It shows options; your prescriber decides.
